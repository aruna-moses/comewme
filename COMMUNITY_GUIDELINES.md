# Come w' me — Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** [DATE]  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** All users of Come w' me (web and mobile)

## 1. Purpose

Come w' me helps people in Lagos find company for real-world activities — market runs, shared rides, food outings, study sessions, and events. These guidelines exist to keep every meetup safe, respectful, and trustworthy.

By creating an account, you agree to follow these guidelines. Violations may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the platform.

## 2. Who Must Follow These Rules

Every registered user, every activity poster, every join requester, and every person sending messages in group chats. Guests browsing the Discover feed are expected to follow the spirit of these rules, but enforcement applies to account holders.

## 3. Core Rules

### A. Meet in Public, Never at Home
- The first meetup must happen at a busy, well-known public place.
- Never share a home address, private office, or residential location in a task title, description, meeting point, or chat.
- Do not pressure others to meet somewhere private.

### B. No Money Up Front
- Nobody should pay to join an activity.
- Do not ask for transfers, deposits, "booking fees," or payment before meeting in person.
- Splitting costs at the actual activity (fare, food, etc.) is between the people involved — Come w' me is not a payment platform.

### C. Be Honest
- Your profile name, photo, age, and location must be real.
- Do not impersonate another person, business, or public figure.
- Activity descriptions must be truthful. Do not post fake events, bait-and-switch listings, or misleading details.

### D. Respect Everyone
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, or intimidation based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.
- No sexual content, nudity, or sexually explicit messages in profiles or chats.
- No bullying, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information without consent.

### E. No Scams or Illegal Activity
- Do not use Come w' me to sell illegal goods, offer illegal services, or promote criminal activity.
- Do not run pyramid schemes, investment scams, or fake giveaways.
- Do not use the app to facilitate underage meetings or exploitation.

### F. Keep It Real
- One account per person. Do not create fake accounts to evade bans or manipulate activity counts.
- Do not spam the feed with duplicate or off-topic postings.

### G. Show Up or Speak Up
- If you accept a spot or join an activity, make every effort to attend.
- If you cannot come, withdraw from the activity or message the group as early as possible.
- Repeated no-shows damage your trust score and may lead to restrictions.

## 4. Content Standards

- **Profile photos:** Must be a clear photo of you. Illustrated or celebrity avatars are not allowed.
- **Activity titles:** Clear, specific, and relevant. No clickbait, all-caps shouting, or unrelated keywords.
- **Chat:** Keep messages on-topic for the activity. No hate speech, threats, or sexual content.

## 5. Reporting and Moderation

If you see a violation:
- Use the **"Report"** button on any activity or profile.
- Reports go to a human reviewer, not an algorithm.
- You can report a user even after an activity is complete.

Reports are reviewed within a reasonable time. You will not be notified of the outcome of every report, but serious violations are acted on quickly.

## 6. Enforcement & Violations

| Violation Level | Examples | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 — Warning** | Minor no-show, first-time rude message, incomplete profile info | In-app warning popup. Must acknowledge before using app. |
| **Level 2 — Temporary Suspension** | Repeated no-shows, harassment, sharing private info, posting fake activity | 24 hours to 7 days suspended. Cannot post or join activities. |
| **Level 3 — Extended Suspension** | Repeated Level 2 offenses, scam attempt, ban evasion attempt | 7 to 30 days suspended. |
| **Level 4 — Permanent Ban** | Sexual content involving minors, fraud, hate speech, threats, criminal activity, repeated severe violations | Permanent removal. Account deleted. |

Severe violations (Level 4) may result in immediate permanent ban without prior warnings.

## 7. Appeals

If you believe a decision was made in error, you can appeal through the **"Appeal"** option in Settings or by emailing hello@comewme.app. Appeals are reviewed by a human. Include your username, the date of the incident, and why you believe the decision was wrong.

## 8. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these guidelines from time to time. When we do, we will notify you in the app and ask you to accept the updated version before continuing.

## 9. Contact

Questions or reports: hello@comewme.app  
Emergencies: Always contact local police first if you feel unsafe.
